## About Lucknow AI Labs

### What is Lucknow AI Labs?

Lucknow AI Labs (LAI) is a volunteer-run community in Lucknow for people who want to learn, build and research AI. It is the first chapter of [UPAI Labs Foundation](https://upailabs.org), a Section 8 not-for-profit company incorporated in March 2025 to grow the AI ecosystem across Uttar Pradesh.

### How are Lucknow AI Labs and AI Community Lucknow related?

They are separate communities that run many events together. AI Community Lucknow was earlier called TFUG Lucknow. Each event on our [Events](/events/) page shows who organised it.

### Who can take part?

Students, developers, researchers, founders and professionals from any field who want to learn about AI or build with it. Beginners are welcome.

### How is Lucknow AI Labs funded?

Lucknow AI Labs is run by volunteers. Partners such as colleges, companies and other communities support individual events, for example with a venue, speakers or refreshments.

### How does Lucknow AI Labs keep its events inclusive?

We want AI education to be open to everyone, whatever their background. Our sessions include material for beginners, and everyone taking part follows our [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/).

### How can I support Lucknow AI Labs?

Come to our events, [volunteer](/volunteer/), [mentor](/mentorship/), give a talk, [contribute code](/projects/) or partner with us on an event. Telling your friends and classmates about us helps too.

## Learning AI

### How can I start studying AI?

Start with Python, which most AI work uses. Then learn the basics of algorithms, data structures and machine learning through online courses, and build small projects as you go.

### How can I apply AI to real-world problems?

Once you know the fundamentals, build projects such as a chatbot, an image classifier or a recommendation system. Kaggle competitions, our [hackathons](/hackathons/) and [open-source projects](/projects/) are good ways to gain experience.

### What careers are there in AI?

Roles include AI engineer, machine learning engineer, data scientist, NLP specialist and AI research scientist. Many industries, from healthcare to finance, hire people with AI skills.

### Which online courses are good for learning AI?

A few well-known starting points:

- Coursera: the Machine Learning Specialization and the Deep Learning Specialization by DeepLearning.AI
- edX: CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python (Harvard)
- fast.ai: Practical Deep Learning for Coders

Our [Resources](/resources/) page has a longer list.

### How much math do I need?

Linear algebra, probability, statistics and calculus help you understand how machine learning works. Many libraries hide the math, so you can start building before you know all of it, and learn the theory alongside.

## Volunteering

### How can I volunteer?

Fill in the form on our [Volunteer](/volunteer/) page or write to [hello@upailabs.org](mailto:hello@upailabs.org).

### Do I need AI or technical skills to volunteer?

No. Technical skills help in some roles, but we also need people for event organisation, outreach, design, writing and administration.

### How much time does it take?

It depends on the role. Some roles need a few hours a week; others are busier around specific events. We will find something that fits your schedule.

### Can I volunteer remotely?

Yes. Many roles, such as content creation, social media and technical support, can be done remotely.

### Can students volunteer?

Yes. Volunteering is a good way for students to learn and get hands-on experience, whatever they study.

### Will I get support as a volunteer?

Yes. Experienced members help new volunteers get started, and you can ask questions on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/QKw67PDZUm) at any time.

### What do I get out of volunteering?

Experience in running a community and its events, new skills, and a network of people working in AI in Lucknow.
