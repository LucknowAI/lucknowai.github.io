# Get involved

Lucknow AI Labs runs on the time and skills of its members. Here is how you can take part.

| If you want to... | Start here |
|:--|:--|
| Attend a meetup, study jam or hackathon | [Events](/events/) and [Commudle](https://www.commudle.com/communities/upai-labs/events) |
| Help organise events, design, write or run our channels | [Volunteer](/volunteer/) |
| Guide a learner, or find a mentor | [Mentorship](/mentorship/) |
| Give a talk or run a workshop | Write to [hello@upailabs.org](mailto:hello@upailabs.org) with your topic |
| Contribute code | [Projects](/projects/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/LucknowAI) |
| Read papers or do research with us | [Research](/research/) |
| Host, sponsor or partner on an event | Write to [hello@upailabs.org](mailto:hello@upailabs.org) |

Everyone who takes part follows our [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/).
