# Mentorship programme

The Lucknow AI Labs mentorship programme pairs experienced AI professionals with learners to work on projects and research together.

## What mentees get

- Guidance from people working in the industry
- Hands-on experience with real AI projects
- A network within the AI community
- Practice with current AI tools and techniques

## What mentors get

- A way to give back to the community
- Recognition as a mentor in Lucknow's AI community
- Practice in leadership and communication

## How it works

**Sessions.** At least one hour a week, with follow-ups on Discord. Sessions happen on Discord and Google Meet.

**Mentors** guide mentees on AI concepts, help them plan and carry out their project, and share career advice.

**Mentees** attend every scheduled session, spend at least 5 hours a week on the programme, write a project proposal in the first two weeks, share a progress update every week and present their project at the end.

All mentor and mentee interactions follow our [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/).
