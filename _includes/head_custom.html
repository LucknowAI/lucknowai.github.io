{%- comment -%} Fonts, and the light and dark themes. The dark scheme (lai) is the theme's default stylesheet. The light scheme (lailight) loads after it and overrides it whenever it applies: by the device setting, or by the visitor's choice from the theme toggle, which is kept in localStorage under "lai-theme". This script runs before the page is drawn, so the chosen theme shows without a flash. {%- endcomment -%}
