# About Lucknow AI Labs

Lucknow AI Labs (LAI) is a volunteer-run community for people who want to learn, build and research AI in Lucknow. Students, working engineers, researchers and founders come to our sessions, build projects in the open and help each other grow.

{: .highlight }
Lucknow AI Labs is the first chapter of **UPAI Labs Foundation** (Uttar Pradesh AI Labs), a Section 8 not-for-profit company incorporated in March 2025 to grow the AI ecosystem across Uttar Pradesh. More at [upailabs.org](https://upailabs.org).

## Why we started

From the note Aaditya (Ankit), one of the founders, wrote when the community began:

> I recently moved to Lucknow and wanted to connect with fellow developers, open-source enthusiasts, and hackers. However, I found that Lucknow lacks such a technology culture and community.
>
> I remember wishing as a college student that I had a mentor to guide me and clarify my doubts. I want the next generation to have those opportunities. So I spoke with friends from Lucknow who now work at various companies and agreed to give back by mentoring youth interested in AI & ML. With that goal, we founded Lucknow AI to advance AI literacy and skills through collaborative workshops, meetups, paper discussions, and community growth.

## How we work

- **Volunteer-run.** Organisers, speakers and mentors give their time outside work and college.
- **Built with partners.** Many events are run together with AI Community Lucknow, GDG Lucknow and colleges across the city. Partners support individual events with venues, speakers or refreshments.
- **Open.** Our [projects](/projects/) are open source, and anyone can contribute.
- **Safe for everyone.** Everyone taking part follows our [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/).

## Who runs it

Lucknow AI Labs is led by its founding team, Ankit Kumar Pal (Aaditya), Prashant Shukla and Abhishek Sahu, who are also the directors of UPAI Labs Foundation, together with a team of volunteers. Meet some of them on the [Team](/team/) page.
